Method and data coverage

What was analysed

  • Mentor database export (537 rows; 535 after removing staff duplicates and the founder) — every profile scored.
  • Notes export: 3,338 Affinity notes, truncated at “next steps / action items” markers and attributed by person, with name-anchored extraction for notes filed under staff. The four XLSX note files referred to in the brief were not uploaded; the CSV export that contains them was used.
  • Enriched LinkedIn-based profiles (542 rows) for career history, founder/exit status and evidence-weighted expertise labels.
  • Granola: three broad queries across mentor calls (HR tech, PLG/SaaS, Nordics/DACH, CFO/fundraising, CHRO/TA).
  • Talentium: both call transcripts, the investment memo, talentium.io, PERKS-FINAL.xlsx.

How scores were built

  • Notes first: theme evidence from notes (≈55–60%), enriched profile labels (≈30–35%) and structured CSV fields (≈10%, with Inovexus-verified industry and skills weighted above Typeform).
  • Founder/exit pass run across all 535 profiles before ranking, so thin-file operators are not beaten by long onboarding notes.
  • Top ~160 candidates, plus every HR-tech hit, reviewed by hand; final DMF is an analyst judgement on fit with the four areas and HR-tech depth.
  • Confidence reflects evidence quality: rich Inovexus notes, active mentoring and onboarded status raise it; self-reported or empty notes and pending onboarding lower it.
